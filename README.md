 Project Overview

The Odoo HR Management System is a personal ERP project developed to demonstrate how an organization can manage employee information and basic HR activities using Odoo.
The system is configured for a software company with 4 departments and 18 employees, including the CEO. It provides a centralized platform to organize employee records, manage skills, maintain department structures, and handle leave requests.

Project Objectives

Organize employee information in one centralized system.
Maintain department and job position details.
Manage employee skills and competencies.
Simplify the employee leave management process.
Provide a clear view of the company's organizational structure.

Business Scenario

This project is based on a software company with the following organizational structure,
4 Departments
18 Employees
1 CEO

The system is designed to support HR activities and improve the way employee information is maintained.

Key Features

1. Employee Management
Create and maintain employee profiles.
Store employee details and job information.
Organize employees according to their departments and positions.

2. Department Management
Configure four company departments.
Assign employees to relevant departments.
Maintain department structures and reporting relationships.

3. Employee Skill Management
Maintain employee skill information.
Record employee competencies.
Support visibility of employee skills and capabilities.

4. Leave Management
Configure employee leave management.
Create and manage leave requests.
View and monitor employee leave information.

5. Organizational Structure
Configure the company hierarchy.
Define job positions and reporting relationships.
Represent the organizational structure, including the CEO and departments.

Tools and Technologies

ERP Platform- Odoo

Module- Employees

Functional Areas- Human Resource Management, Employee Management, Leave Management

Project Type- Personal ERP Configuration Project
